CardMinder is a 100% offline, privacy-first Flutter application designed to track credit and debit card inactivity timelines and prevent unexpected bank deactivations.

Banks often deactivate credit or debit cards if no transaction is made within a set period (e.g. 3, 6, 9 months or 1 year). CardMinder keeps all your card timelines organized in one place with countdown indicators, home screen widgets, and automated local notification reminders.

Key Features:
• 100% Offline & Private: No internet permission required. All data stays strictly on your device.
• Custom Deactivation Timelines: Track cards with 3 months, 6 months, 9 months, or 1 year inactivity deadlines.
• One-Tap Reset: Easily mark a transaction as used today to reset your deadline.
• Home Screen Widgets: Keep track of upcoming deadlines right from your home screen.
• Local Notification Reminders: Customizable transaction reminders before deadlines expire.
• Encrypted Backup & Restore: PIN-protected backup and restore functionality.
• Clean & Modern UI: Full Light and Dark mode support with sleek card designs.
